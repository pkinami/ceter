## 1. Introduction

Ceter Technologies Limited ("**Ceter**", "**we**", "**us**", "**our**") supplies office printing equipment, consumables, spare parts, and related IT services and solutions to individuals, businesses, institutions, and government entities in Kenya, through our website at cetertechnologies.com (the "**Site**"), WhatsApp, and our physical premises.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have over it. It is written to comply with the Data Protection Act, No. 24 of 2019, the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, and related subsidiary legislation of Kenya.

By using the Site, creating an account, placing an order, requesting a quotation, or otherwise providing us with personal data, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

## 4. Information We Collect

### 4.1 Information you give us directly

- **Account information:** name, email address, phone number, delivery and billing address, and a password (stored in encrypted/hashed form — we never store your password in plain text).
- **Order and quotation information:** products or services ordered or enquired about, quantities, delivery instructions, purchase order or LPO references, and — for institutional and government clients — organisation name, procurement contact details, and KRA PIN where required for invoicing.
- **Payment information:** for M-Pesa payments, the transaction reference and phone number used; for card payments, our third-party payment processor handles and stores your card details directly — we do not store full card numbers on our own systems. For institutional clients on invoice terms, bank transfer references.
- **Communications:** messages you send us through the Site, WhatsApp, email, phone, or in person, including for customer support, warranty claims, or service requests.
- **Service and warranty records:** equipment serial numbers, installation and service history, and warranty status, where you purchase equipment or a maintenance contract from us.

### 4.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, we (and service providers acting on our behalf) may automatically collect:

- IP address, browser type and version, device type, and operating system;
- pages viewed, time spent on the Site, and referring website;
- cookies and similar technologies, as described in Section 6.

### 4.3 Information from third parties

- Payment confirmation from our payment service providers (for example, confirmation that an M-Pesa or card payment was successful).
- Delivery status from courier or logistics partners engaged to deliver your order.
- Publicly available business information (for example, from a company's public procurement profile) where we are responding to a formal tender.

## 6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies to operate correctly and to understand how it is used.

- **Strictly necessary cookies** — required for core functions such as keeping you signed in, remembering items in your cart, and maintaining session security. These cannot be switched off without affecting the Site's functionality.
- **Analytics cookies** — where enabled (for example, Google Analytics), these help us understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it. Data collected is generally aggregated and does not directly identify you.
- **Advertising cookies** — where we run advertising through platforms such as Google Ads, these may be used to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. We do not currently sell or share your data with advertisers for their own independent marketing purposes.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the Site, such as the cart and checkout, from working correctly. Where we use Google Analytics or Google Ads, those services are governed by Google's own privacy practices, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

## 7. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share personal data only where necessary, with:

- **Payment service providers**, to process M-Pesa, card, and bank payments;
- **Delivery and logistics partners**, to fulfil and deliver your order;
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers**, who store and process data on our behalf under contractual data protection obligations (see Section 8 on international transfers);
- **Manufacturers and authorised distributors**, where necessary to process a warranty claim or enrich a product listing with manufacturer-supplied specifications and images;
- **Professional advisers**, such as our auditors, accountants, or lawyers, where necessary and under confidentiality obligations;
- **Government and regulatory authorities**, including the Kenya Revenue Authority, where required by law, or law enforcement, where legally compelled;
- **A buyer or successor**, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to that party agreeing to honour this Policy in respect of personal data already collected.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Our Site is hosted using third-party infrastructure providers, which may store or process data outside Kenya, including in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, we take steps required under the Data Protection Act, 2019 to ensure it remains protected, including relying on the provider's contractual data protection commitments, and, where applicable, confirming the recipient country or organisation is subject to an adequate level of data protection, or that appropriate contractual safeguards are in place.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy:

- **Account data** is retained for as long as your account remains active, and for a reasonable period after closure in case you wish to reactivate it.
- **Order, invoice, and payment records** are retained for at least five years from the end of the relevant financial year, in line with record-keeping obligations under Kenyan tax law.
- **Warranty and service records** are retained for the duration of the applicable warranty or service contract, and for a reasonable period afterward to support any related claim.
- **Marketing data** is retained until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe.

Where data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it, except where we are required by law to retain it for longer.

## 10. Data Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data we hold, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS), encrypted or hashed storage of passwords, restricted staff access to personal data on a need-to-know basis, and role-based access controls within our systems. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we review our safeguards on an ongoing basis.

If we become aware of a data breach affecting your personal data that is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner and affected individuals as required under the Data Protection Act, 2019.

## 11. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

- be informed of how your personal data is being used;
- access the personal data we hold about you;
- request correction of inaccurate or outdated personal data;
- request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal obligation to retain certain records (such as tax and accounting records);
- object to, or request that we restrict, processing of your personal data in certain circumstances;
- request a copy of your personal data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent (for example, marketing communications), without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal;
- lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (see Section 17).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 16. We may need to verify your identity before actioning a request, and will respond within the timeframes required by law.

## 12. Children's Privacy

The Site and our services are directed at businesses, institutions, and adult consumers, and are not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 years of age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.
